The binding rules

These carry the strategic line into every screen, several inherited from the plan's behavioral constraints, all expressed in the palette.

The One Voice Rule
Amber on at most 10% of any screen, only meaning "what's next / active." Primary buttons are Indigo, not Amber.
The No-Alarm Rule
Overdue, slipping, and stale never use Errors-red or Rose. Red is destructive-confirm only. Time pressure speaks in Secondary-text gray, placement, and plain words.
The Inferred Ink Rule
Inferred values are gray with a dotted underline + "inferred" tag; confirmed values are solid Slate with an Indigo check. No success-green; trust is never hue alone.
The Two-Voice Rule
Aptos Sans operates, Aptos Serif narrates. A serif label on a control is forbidden.
The Provenance Inset Rule
Every verbatim source quote sits in a tinted inset, italic, distinct from the model's paraphrase.
The Flat & Precise Rule
Flat at rest, hairline borders, small radius. Shadow only on things that truly float: the picker, popovers, modals.
